These additions retain Solar's path geometry, centered and scaled to the
pack's 19-unit drawing area. Colour uses the existing pastel palette and
0.75-unit black outlines; Mono uses unfilled shapes and 1.0-unit black outlines.
Menu Dots and Tablet are also rotated 90 degrees to match their ZenOS controls.

library.svg is pxlflux's adapted bookshelf.svg, renamed for ZenOS's Library.
tab_series.svg uses the same book paths rotated -90 degrees into a horizontal stack.
All original KOReader filenames and artwork are retained in the grouped source folders.
Additional lookup aliases are regular source SVGs sharing the corresponding artwork
without further modifications. The Python builder copies these unchanged into a flat
pack folder. No scripts or filename mapping are needed at runtime.
ZenPM's bundled SVG names and inline menu names are included as lookup aliases;
its frontend must resolve the selected ZenOS icon pack to display them.
